parser: the shared parser reads month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 2 December lands on 12 February. This is the problem the #4471 page describes, and the queued fix covers it. Tracked in umarvinci/support-kb#1.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Its tenants write the day first, so their people type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form then reads the date day first. The swap happens in the form, before the parser sees the date. The cause table on the #4471 page already marks these reports “not this ticket”; they had no ticket of their own until umarvinci/support-kb#2.

shift: a date moves by one day

Some reports describe a date moved by one day, or one meeting on different days for people in different places: Tuesday in Lisbon, Monday in Toronto. A month/day swap can’t do that. The desk noted these as one-day moves in desk-notes.json but filed them under parser by tenant. They point at time zones; the cause isn’t confirmed yet. Tracked in umarvinci/support-kb#3.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. Every problem here produces the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Month and day swapped?”), so the subject can’t tell them apart.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant, so it doesn’t separate them either.
  • What separates them is where a report comes from (its tenant and booking form) and what it says happened (a swap, or a one-day move). problems.ts makes that split, and the table above is built from it when the page renders.
